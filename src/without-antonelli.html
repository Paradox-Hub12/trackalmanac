How this works

For every Grand Prix and Sprint with a published result on Track Almanac, remove Antonelli and move each classified finisher behind him up one place. Award Grand Prix points of 25–18–15–12–10–8–6–4–2–1 and Sprint points of 8–7–6–5–4–3–2–1. Unclassified finishers receive no points. Wins count Grands Prix only.

This is arithmetic, not a prediction: strategy, incidents, retirements, qualifying and the behaviour of other drivers are not resimulated. Points gained by other drivers need not equal Antonelli's original total. Ties use Grand Prix finishing-position countback; unresolved ties share a position.

Remaining points are the maximum one driver can score from Grands Prix and Sprints still awaiting results, using the site's calendar and session schedule. A driver within that total of the leader can still catch or tie them on points; this does not resolve future countback ties.
