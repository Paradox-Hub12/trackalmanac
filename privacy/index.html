Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information Track Almanac ("we", "us") collects when you visit trackalmanac.com (the "Site"), how it is used and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply.
  • Preferences stored on your device. The Site saves small settings in your browser's local storage, such as your 12/24-hour clock choice, kilometres or miles, light or dark theme, race card order and cookie choice. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Automatically collected information. Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising partners may receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and referring page.

2. Cookies and advertising

We may use third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, to keep the Site free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before non-essential cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google, to count visits and see which pages are popular, so we know what to improve.

  • What it collects. The pages you view and their titles, the site that referred you, approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address; Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses), device type, browser, operating system, screen size and language, and basic interactions such as scrolling, time on page and clicks on links to other sites. It does not collect your name or email address.
  • Cookies. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*) to tell visits apart. They can last up to two years.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics cookies are only set if you choose Accept all. Until then, or if you choose Essential only, no analytics cookies are stored; Google may receive limited signals without cookies or identifiers (for example, that a page was viewed), which it uses only in aggregate. Elsewhere, analytics cookies are used by default and you can turn them off by choosing Essential only.
  • Changing your choice. Use the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page at any time.
  • More information and opting out. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also block Google Analytics on every website with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

3a. Predictor game

The Track Almanac Predictor is an optional, free prediction game. You can read the rules and the leaderboards without an account. Only when you save picks do you sign in, in one of three ways, and we store:

  • Sign-in details. With Google: your email address and your Google account's name and ID, as provided by Google. With email and password: your email address; the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it. As a guest: no email address, only a random account ID kept in your browser.
  • Your game profile. The display name you choose, an optional country, your age confirmation and when you joined.
  • Your picks and scores, and the leagues you create or join.

Who stores it. Sign-in and game data are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google as our service provider; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. Firebase may process data outside your country.

Why. To run the game: to save your picks, score them, and show leaderboards and league tables. This is necessary to provide the game you asked to play. We don't use game data for advertising and we don't sell it.

Guests. A guest account lives in your browser only. If you clear your browser's data, sign out or switch devices, you lose access to it and its picks. Use Save my account on the Account page to link it to Google or an email address and keep everything. Guests appear on the leaderboards with a "Guest" tag.

Email confirmation. If you sign up with email and password, we send you one email to confirm the address, and you appear on the leaderboards once it is confirmed. We also send a password reset email when you ask for one. We send no other emails.

What others can see. Your display name, points and rank are public on the leaderboards. League members see each other's display names and scores. After a round's deadline, other signed-in players may see your picks for that round. Your email address is never shown.

How long. We keep your game data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time on the Account page: this removes your profile, display name, picks and league memberships at once, and your scores disappear from the published tables at the next update. Tables already published for past rounds are replaced when they are next rebuilt.

Sign-in storage. Signing in (also as a guest) keeps a sign-in token in your browser's storage. It is essential for the game and is not used for analytics or advertising; your cookie choice for analytics and ads is unaffected.

Age. You must be 13 or older to play (16 or older in the EEA or the UK).

4. How we use information

To operate and improve the Site, keep it secure, show advertising that funds it, and respond to messages. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents may opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@trackalmanac.com.

6. Children

The Site is a general-audience sports reference and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and security

We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record keeping. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@trackalmanac.com.
