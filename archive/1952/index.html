Season archive

1952 Formula 1 season

With the championship run to Formula 2 rules, Alberto Ascari won six races in a row for Ferrari and took the title by 12 points.

  • Formula 2 regulations were used in 1952 and 1953, after Alfa Romeo's withdrawal left too few Formula 1 cars.
  • Fangio missed the season after breaking his neck in a non-championship race at Monza.
