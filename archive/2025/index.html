Season archive

2025 Formula 1 season

McLaren had the fastest car for most of the year and Oscar Piastri led the standings through the summer. Verstappen mounted a late charge in a slower Red Bull, turning it into a three-way fight that went to Abu Dhabi, where Norris took his first title by two points.

  • McLaren clinched the constructors' title with six rounds to spare.
  • Hamilton's first season at Ferrari ended without a Grand Prix win.
  • Final year of the 2022–2025 ground-effect rules.
