Season archive

2015 Formula 1 season

Mercedes won 16 of 19 races. Hamilton took ten wins and wrapped up his third title with three races left at the United States GP, while Vettel won three races in his first year with Ferrari.

  • Max Verstappen made his debut at 17.
  • McLaren's reunion with Honda brought only 27 points.
  • Rosberg won the last three races of the year.
