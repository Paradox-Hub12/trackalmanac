Season archive

1994 Formula 1 season

Michael Schumacher won his first title, by one point from Damon Hill, after the two collided at the final race in Adelaide.

  • Ayrton Senna and Roland Ratzenberger were killed at Imola.
  • Schumacher was disqualified twice and banned for two races.
