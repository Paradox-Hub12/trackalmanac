Season archive

1960 Formula 1 season

Brabham and Cooper retained both titles, with Brabham winning five races in a row in the middle of the season.

  • It was the last year the Indianapolis 500 counted towards the championship.
  • Stirling Moss gave Lotus its first championship win, at Monaco, in Rob Walker's privately entered car.
