Season archive

1978 Formula 1 season

Mario Andretti won the title in the ground-effect Lotus 79, with his team-mate Ronnie Peterson second.

  • Peterson died after a crash at the start of the Italian Grand Prix.
  • Brabham's 'fan car' won in Sweden and was then withdrawn.
