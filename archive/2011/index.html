Season archive

2011 Formula 1 season

Vettel and Red Bull dominated, with 11 wins and a record 15 poles. He clinched his second title with four races to spare in Japan.

  • DRS and Pirelli tyres arrived, along with the return of KERS.
  • Button won a rain-hit Canadian GP from last place after six pit stops.
  • Red Bull's second straight constructors' title.
