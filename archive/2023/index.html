2023 Formula 1 season

The most dominant season in F1 history. Red Bull won 21 of 22 Grands Prix, with Carlos Sainz's Singapore victory the only exception, and Verstappen set records for wins in a season and consecutive wins.

  • Verstappen won 10 races in a row.
  • Las Vegas returned to the calendar on the Strip.
  • Sprint format expanded to six weekends.
