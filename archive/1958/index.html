Season archive

1958 Formula 1 season

Mike Hawthorn became the first British world champion, by a single point from Stirling Moss, despite winning only one race.

  • The constructors' championship was awarded for the first time, to Vanwall.
  • Moss spoke up for Hawthorn when he faced disqualification in Portugal, which proved decisive.
  • Peter Collins and Stuart Lewis-Evans were killed during the season.
