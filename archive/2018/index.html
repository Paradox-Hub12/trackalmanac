Season archive

2018 Formula 1 season

Vettel and Ferrari led the early part of the season, but a crash from the lead at Hockenheim and a run of errors let Hamilton pull clear. Hamilton won 11 races and sealed his fifth title in Mexico.

  • The halo cockpit protection was introduced.
  • Räikkönen won in Austin, his first win since 2013.
  • Fernando Alonso left F1 at the end of the season.
