Season archive

2009 Formula 1 season

Brawn GP, rescued from Honda's withdrawal, won six of the first seven races with Jenson Button and took both titles in its only season.

  • New aerodynamic rules, slick tyres and KERS shook up the order.
  • Double diffusers gave Brawn, Toyota and Williams an early-season advantage.
  • Red Bull won its first Grand Prix, in China, with Sebastian Vettel.
