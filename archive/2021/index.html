2021 Formula 1 season

Verstappen and Hamilton fought all year, with collisions at Silverstone and Monza. They arrived in Abu Dhabi level on points, and a disputed safety car restart on the final lap gave Verstappen his first title.

  • Mercedes won an eighth straight constructors' title.
  • Sprint qualifying debuted at Silverstone.
  • Race director Michael Masi was replaced after the finale.
