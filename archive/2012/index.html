Season archive

2012 Formula 1 season

Seven different drivers won the first seven races. Alonso led for much of the year in a Ferrari that was not the fastest car, but Vettel won four in a row in the autumn and took the title by three points in a wet Brazilian finale.

  • Eight different race winners.
  • Kimi Räikkönen won in Abu Dhabi on his return with Lotus.
  • Vettel became the youngest triple world champion.
