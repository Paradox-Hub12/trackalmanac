1950 Formula 1 season

The first Formula 1 World Championship. Alfa Romeo won every race it entered, and Nino Farina took the title by three points from his team-mate Juan Manuel Fangio.

  • The championship began at Silverstone on 13 May 1950.
  • The Indianapolis 500 counted towards the championship from 1950 to 1960, though few regular drivers took part.
  • Points went to the top five finishers, plus one for the fastest lap.
