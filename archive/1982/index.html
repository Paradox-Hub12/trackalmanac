Season archive

1982 Formula 1 season

Keke Rosberg won the title for Williams with a single victory, in a season with 11 different winners.

  • Gilles Villeneuve was killed in qualifying at Zolder, and Riccardo Paletti at the start in Canada.
  • Didier Pironi led the championship until a crash in practice at Hockenheim ended his career.
