Season archive

1954 Formula 1 season

Fangio won his second title. He started the season with Maserati, then moved to the returning Mercedes-Benz team and won on its debut at Reims.

  • Mercedes returned with the W196, raced with streamlined bodywork at fast circuits.
  • New 2.5-litre engine rules began.
