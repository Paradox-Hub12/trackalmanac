1951 Formula 1 season

Juan Manuel Fangio won the first of his five titles for Alfa Romeo, holding off Ferrari's Alberto Ascari at the final race in Spain.

  • José Froilán González gave Ferrari its first championship win, at Silverstone.
  • It was Alfa Romeo's last season before withdrawing from Grand Prix racing.
