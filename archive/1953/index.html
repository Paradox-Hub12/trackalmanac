1953 Formula 1 season

Ascari retained the title for Ferrari with five wins, ahead of Fangio, now driving for Maserati.

  • Ascari's run of nine consecutive championship wins, from Belgium 1952 to Belgium 1953, stood as the record for 70 years.
  • Mike Hawthorn beat Fangio by a second at Reims to become the first British driver to win a championship Grand Prix.
