Season archive

2005 Formula 1 season

Fernando Alonso and Renault ended Ferrari's five-year run. Alonso became the youngest world champion at 24, holding off McLaren's Kimi Räikkönen, who won seven races.

  • Tyre changes during races were banned for the season.
  • Only six cars started the United States Grand Prix after the Michelin-shod teams withdrew.
  • Red Bull Racing replaced Jaguar on the grid.
