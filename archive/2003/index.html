Season archive

2003 Formula 1 season

The closest title fight in years. A new points system kept Räikkönen in contention until the final race at Suzuka, where Schumacher took eighth place and a record sixth title by two points.

  • Points were now awarded to the top eight: 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1.
  • Fernando Alonso became the youngest Grand Prix winner at the time, in Hungary.
  • Single-lap qualifying and parc fermé between qualifying and the race were introduced.
