Season archive

2000 Formula 1 season

Michael Schumacher won nine races and ended Ferrari's 21-year wait for a drivers' champion, beating Mika Häkkinen after a season-long duel with McLaren. He sealed the title at Suzuka with a race to spare.

  • Ferrari's first drivers' title since Jody Scheckter in 1979, and Schumacher's third world title.
  • McLaren lost 10 constructors' points at the Austrian Grand Prix over a missing seal on Häkkinen's car.
  • Jaguar replaced Stewart on the grid; Jenson Button made his debut with Williams.
