Season archive

2022 Formula 1 season

Ground-effect rules arrived and Ferrari started strongest, with Leclerc winning two of the first three races. Reliability failures and strategy errors let Red Bull take over, and Verstappen clinched his second title in Japan.

  • First year of the ground-effect regulations.
  • Miami joined the calendar.
  • Mercedes won only one race, in Brazil.
