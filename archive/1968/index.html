Season archive

1968 Formula 1 season

Graham Hill led a grieving Lotus to both titles after Jim Clark was killed in a Formula 2 race at Hockenheim in April.

  • Wings appeared on Formula 1 cars for the first time.
  • Lotus raced in Gold Leaf colours, beginning the era of sponsors' liveries.
