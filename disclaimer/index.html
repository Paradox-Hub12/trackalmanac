We do not use official F1, team or sponsor logos, photographs or broadcast imagery. Team colours are approximations used to tell teams apart in tables and charts. Circuit maps are drawn from open car telemetry data and are not official track maps.

Data sources and credits

  • Race classifications, qualifying, practice, starting grids and session times: compiled from official published results and timing.
  • Results, qualifying, sprint results, championship standings and the race schedule: Jolpica F1 API (successor to the Ergast API).
  • Lap-by-lap positions: TracingInsights timing data (Apache-2.0), and OpenF1 for new races.
  • Practice and sprint qualifying classifications, lap timing, safety car, virtual safety car and red flag periods, and session times: OpenF1 (openf1.org).
  • Tyre strategy: TracingInsights, from the official F1 timing feed (Apache-2.0).
  • Circuit maps: drawn from TracingInsights car telemetry (Apache-2.0).
  • Past seasons (archive, 1950–2025): F1DB, CC BY 4.0.
  • Grand Prix racing 1946–1949: the 1946–1949 Grand Prix season pages on Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Country flags: flag-icons, MIT licence.
  • Tyre compound selections and weekend forecasts: published team and supplier race previews.
  • Fonts: Big Shoulders Display, IBM Plex Sans Condensed and IBM Plex Mono via Google Fonts (SIL Open Font Licence).
