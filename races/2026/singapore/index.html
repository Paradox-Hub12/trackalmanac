Round 17 of 23 · 9–11 Oct

Singapore Grand Prix 2026

Marina Bay Street Circuit, Singapore, Singapore. 62 laps of 4.927 km (305.5 km).

Max Verstappen won the Singapore Grand Prix 2026 for Red Bull Racing, ahead of Charles Leclerc and Kimi Antonelli. Pole position: Max Verstappen. Fastest lap: Charles Leclerc, 1:35.202 on lap 41. Verstappen converted pole into his first Singapore win, completing a Sprint and Grand Prix double: he squeezed Leclerc at Turn 1 and kept the lead through his only stop. Leclerc took second by passing Hamilton on lap 46, and Antonelli climbed to third late on, passing Norris and Hamilton, while Hamilton, who had switched to softs early, faded to sixth. Russell recovered from 19th on the grid to ninth. Antonelli now leads Russell by 102 points and can clinch the title in Austin.

Starting grid

Penalties and pit lane starts

Russell starts from the back after a 40-place grid penalty for using additional power unit elements. Alonso starts from the pit lane after Aston Martin changed his turbocharger under parc fermé. Stroll starts from the pit lane for a parc fermé breach, as his car was still uncovered in the garage after the post-qualifying time limit. Hadjar starts from the pit lane after Red Bull changed his engine, exhaust and turbocharger; he had set no time in qualifying.
