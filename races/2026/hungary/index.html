Hungarian Grand Prix 2026

Hungaroring, Mogyoród, Hungary. 70 laps of 4.381 km (306.7 km).

Lando Norris won the Hungarian Grand Prix 2026 for McLaren, ahead of Max Verstappen and Kimi Antonelli. Pole position: Lando Norris. Fastest lap: Charles Leclerc, 1:22.000 on lap 58. 19 classified; Piastri, Pérez and Bottas retired.
