Round 21 of 23 · 19–21 Nov

Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026

Las Vegas Strip Circuit, Las Vegas, United States. 50 laps of 6.201 km (310.0 km).

Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026 for Red Bull Racing, ahead of Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri. Pole position: Kimi Antonelli. Fastest lap: Liam Lawson, 1:35.591 on lap 50. Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix from the front of the grid, 0.639 seconds ahead of Lando Norris, with Oscar Piastri third for a McLaren double podium. Kimi Antonelli, fastest in qualifying but sixth on the grid after an engine penalty, ran second until a late problem dropped him to fourth. George Russell hit the barrier at Turn 5 on lap 48 and fell to ninth. Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc each received a 10-second penalty, Hamilton for gaining an advantage off track and Leclerc for colliding with his team-mate, and finished seventh and tenth. Alexander Albon did not start after his brakes overheated on the way to the grid.

Starting grid

Penalties and pit lane starts

Antonelli was fastest in qualifying but takes a five-place grid penalty for an extra internal combustion engine, so he starts 6th and Verstappen inherits pole. Piastri drops ten places from 7th to 17th for a fifth internal combustion engine. Sainz takes a ten-place penalty for a fifth turbocharger and starts 20th. Stroll takes a ten-place penalty for a new energy store and control electronics and starts 21st. Bearman starts last after Haas fitted several new power unit elements (35 places). Lawson started from the pit lane after Racing Bulls repaired a fuel leak, leaving 7th place empty. Albon did not start after his brakes overheated on the way to the grid.
