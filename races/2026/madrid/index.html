Round 14 of 23 · 13 September 2026

Spanish Grand Prix 2026

Madring, Madrid, Spain. 57 laps of 5.474 km (312.0 km).

Kimi Antonelli won the Spanish Grand Prix 2026 for Mercedes, ahead of Max Verstappen and Lando Norris. Pole position: Lando Norris. Fastest lap: George Russell, 1:35.587 on lap 49. First race at the new Madrid street circuit. Sainz, Pérez, Stroll and Hamilton retired.
