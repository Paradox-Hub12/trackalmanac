Bahrain Grand Prix 2026

Sepang International Circuit, Sepang, Malaysia, Malaysia. 55 laps of 5.543 km (304.9 km).

Max Verstappen won the Bahrain Grand Prix 2026 for Red Bull Racing, ahead of Kimi Antonelli and Lewis Hamilton. Pole position: Max Verstappen. Fastest lap: Max Verstappen, 1:38.220 on lap 55. Moved from April and relocated to Sepang, F1's first visit to Malaysia in nine years. Verstappen's first win of 2026, from pole.
