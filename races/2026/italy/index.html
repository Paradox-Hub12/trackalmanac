Round 13 of 23 · 6 September 2026

Italian Grand Prix 2026

Autodromo Nazionale Monza, Monza, Italy. 53 laps of 5.793 km (307.0 km).

Kimi Antonelli won the Italian Grand Prix 2026 for Mercedes, ahead of George Russell and Max Verstappen. Pole position: Pierre Gasly. Fastest lap: Kimi Antonelli, 1:23.504 on lap 53. Antonelli won from 19th on the grid after a power unit penalty, the first Italian to win at Monza since 1966. Leclerc crashed on lap 2, bringing a red flag.
