Bugesera circuit (Confirmed)

Early 2030s: A new purpose-built circuit about 40 km south of Kigali, next to the new Kigali International Airport. Formula 1 announced a 10-year agreement for an African Grand Prix on 13 October 2026, F1's first race in Africa since Kyalami in 1993. The first race follows the circuit's completion; no date yet.

Bangkok street circuit (Bid)

2028 (planned): Thailand's government has approved a bid for a 5.7 km, 18-corner street circuit around Chatuchak in northern Bangkok, aiming for a first race in 2028 on an initial five-year deal. As of July 2026 there was no signed race agreement.

Catalunya (Returning)

2028: Off the 2027 calendar. Barcelona is due back in 2028, sharing a slot with Spa-Francorchamps, the two alternating year by year.
